Lasagna in 25 mins!

Description

Its an Italian 🇮🇹 dish, perfect to cook for many people, and obiously DELICIOUS!

Ingredients

  • Pasta blankets
  • 300ml Tomato paste
  • 500g Beef meat
  • Mixed grained cheese
  • 200ml Milk
  • 100g Flour

Steps

  1. Cook the beef on a pan until seared
  2. Add the tomato paste
  3. In another casserole put the Milk and Flour in and steer until it creates a paste
  4. In an Oven pan add the beef then the pasta sheets, then the beef and so on making layers
  5. Once cooked add the cheese and grill for 5 mins
