Carbonara

Description

Its an Italian 🇮🇹 dish, perfect to cook for many people, and obiously DELICIOUS!

Ingredients

  • Spagetti
  • Two egg yolks
  • 50g Guanchiale
  • 50g Pecorino cheese
  • Black Pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the guanchiale on a pan until crispy LOW HEAT!
  2. In a bowl mix the egg yolks, pecorino and black pepper
  3. Once the pasta is cooked reserve some water and add the spaggeti to the guanchiale pan
  4. Add the sauce and mix with no heat
